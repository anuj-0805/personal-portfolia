Exploration & Discovery

My AI Journey

How a simple conversation with ChatGPT ignited my curiosity into how AI models learn, what they know about us, and how privacy shapes the experience.

Step 01

The Spark of Curiosity

I initiated a conversation with ChatGPT to find out what information it actually had about me. Instead of making assumptions, I asked direct questions to test its awareness, memory capabilities, and perception.

💡 Key Takeaway: AI only knows what we actively tell it within the conversation.
Step 02

Uncovering Privacy & Boundaries

I discovered that personal details like my exact age, school, job, address, or private photos were never accessible to the AI. The system respected privacy boundaries and operated with its memory feature disabled, reinforcing trust in responsible AI design.

🔒 Key Takeaway: Privacy by design is essential for ethical human-AI collaboration.
Step 03

Understanding Personalization

Even without storing private records, the model adapted to my communication style — noticing my casual phrasing, my preference for concise texting, and my curiosity-driven questions. This highlighted how intelligent systems can provide relevant, friendly interactions while honoring privacy.

🎯 Key Takeaway: True personalization comes from real-time context, not invasive tracking.
Step 04

From Insight to Building

Inspired by this exploration, I decided to build a tangible project: this personal portfolio. Using Google Antigravity and VS Code Live Server, I transformed these ideas into a fully responsive, modern web experience.

🚀 Key Takeaway: Knowledge is most valuable when you use it to create something new.
